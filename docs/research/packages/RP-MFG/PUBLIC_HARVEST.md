## Jetstream (public safety manual)

| Asset | URL |
|-------|-----|
| General Waterblast Safety Manual PDF | https://waterblast.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/JS-General-Waterblast-Safety-Manual.pdf |

**Public claims of note [CITATION]:**
- Noise often > OSHA 90 dB(A) — hearing protection required.  
- Thrust/rearward force substantial; manual often limited around **~50 lbf** or **1/3 operator weight** — **aligns MCR-016** three additive controls (250 N ≈ 56 lbf is same family).  
- Brace footing before trigger; nozzle sizing controls thrust.  
- Use only HP-rated fittings (not brass/cast improvised).  
- Injection injury: seek medical attention immediately; inform medical staff of mechanism.

**Alignment:** MCR-016/017, MCR-007/008 fittings, Ch10 medical.
