### SF 18/20 detail extract (public page) [CITATION]

- Pressure: **250 bar** high-pressure washer on dock floor (thruster grating).  
- Operator kick-back on trigger; stepped back; lance turned; sprayed supervisor behind.  
- Causes: no barriers; supervisor failed to enforce TRA barriers; supervisor placed self in line of fire.  
- Actions: barriers at safe distance; supervisors are **non-essential** during HP task; dock-bottom access control; competency + PPE rated to pressure.
