## Class A / Class B (public definition)

Used widely in AU WHS practice (WorkSafe Victoria, 14 Sep 2022):

| Class | System output (bar × L/min) | Characterisation (public) |
|-------|-----------------------------|---------------------------|
| **A** | **800 – 5 600** | Lower energy class within regulated HPWJ range |
| **B** | **> 5 600** | Higher powered; higher operator risk; stricter training path |

**Scope note (public secondary):** Standards generally address positive-displacement systems with capability **> 800 bar·L/min**.

**Anabeeb note:** Anabeeb classifies by pressure bands + **MCR-046 ceiling**, not by bar·L/min class. Use AU classes for **international comparison** only unless human maps and adopts.

## Training & competency (public)

| Requirement | Public source summary |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Competent personnel only to operate/supervise | AS/NZS themes via WorkSafe VIC / SWA |
| Class B: RTO training + assessment to HPWJ units of competency | WorkSafe VIC |
| Class B trainers: RTO-accredited + **≥5 years** relevant water jetting experience | WorkSafe VIC |
| Refresher / competency reassessment **at least every 2 years**, recorded | WorkSafe VIC; SWA guide practice |
| Extra training when new/more powerful equipment or technology | WorkSafe VIC |

## PPE themes (WorkSafe VIC public)

- Personal eye protection near jetting operations  
- Person controlling flow: **minimum** safety glasses **and** face shield  
- Head protection where needed  
- Leg/body armour of material that can stand direct jet force where injury risk exists  
- Hearing protection where noise not controlled
