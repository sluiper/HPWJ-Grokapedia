## Public medical extract (fluid injection) [CITATION]

From WJTA Safety page (attributes Orange Book §4.1.1, 2nd ed. citation on page):

- All fluid injection injuries have potential to be severe.  
- Team members should carry an easily accessible card outlining the nature of the injury.  
- Evaluation/management should **parallel gunshot injury**.  
- External manifestations **cannot** predict internal damage.  
- Stabilize; thorough neurovascular exam; imaging for subcutaneous air / foreign bodies.  
- Extremity risk: nerve/muscle/vessel damage, compartment syndrome.  
- Torso risk: internal organ damage.  
- Surgical consultation; aggressive irrigation/debridement; possible decompression/exploration.  
- Broad-spectrum IV antibiotics; cultures; **water source** informs empiric therapy.  

**Anabeeb alignment:** MCR-043 Medical Alert + Ch10 emergency pathway. Field rule remains: treat as emergency until physician clears.

## Hydroblaster Training and Certification (public structure)

### Foundational Training (FT – 19HBFT)
- Computer-based (~3 h) overview aligned to Best Practices  
- Exam (public materials cite ~80% pass)  
- Validity often **3 years** on public materials  
- Delivery via HASC / ARSC / proctoring options  

### Field Verification (FV – 19HBFV)
- Hands-on skills verification by WJTA Verified Trainer  
- System setup, pre-job inspection, shotgunning, flex lancing, line moleing; automated methods included in updated curriculum  
- FT is prerequisite  

### Verified Trainer / Training Site
- Train-the-trainer + audit model for consistency  

**Anabeeb use:** Practical FV structure maps to OP-001 Field Verification checklist discipline. Numeric thresholds still come from **MCR**, not Orange Book (full text not in repo).
