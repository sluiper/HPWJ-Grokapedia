## Class A / Class B (public language — WorkSafe Victoria)

| Class | Output capability (bar · L/min) | Training note (public) |
|-------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| **A** | 800 – 5 600 | Competent by competent person; compatible fittings ≥ MAWP |
| **B** | **> 5 600** | Higher risk; **RTO** training + assessment; trainers RTO-accredited + **≥5 years** relevant experience |

**System scope (public secondary):** AS/NZS applies positive-displacement systems generally above **800 bar·L/min**.

---

## Risk list (WorkSafe Victoria public)

- Water jet pierce skin  
- Contaminated material injection  
- Flying debris (incl. eyes)  
- Flailing hose / out-of-control nozzle / coupling separation  
- Noise  
- Confined space  
- Falls  
- Chemical / biological exposure  
- Electric shock

## Controls themes (public)

| Theme | Public guidance summary | MCR alignment (existing) |
|-------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| PPE | Eye protection always near jetting; flow controller min safety glasses + face shield; body armour where jet-force injury risk; hearing protection | Ch7/OPS PPE matrix |
| Training | Competent person; Class B via RTO units of competency | MCR-030, 042 |
| Refresh | Competency/refresher **at least every 2 years** recorded | Ch13 24-month VOC |
| Modification | No operator mods without manufacturer/competent written approval; Class B mods need engineering competence | MCR-015 culture |
| Hose/fittings | Right size, compatible, rated ≥ max operating pressure | MCR-001–008 |
