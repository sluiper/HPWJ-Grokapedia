## Training pathway (public structure)

City & Guilds accreditation referenced on WJA and provider pages.

### 1. Water Jetting Safety Awareness (entry theory)
- Classroom theory (commonly ~1 day at providers)  
- Hazards (including injection), risk assessment, PPE, emergency response, CoP orientation  
- Examined pass before practical pathway elements  

### 2. Practical modules
- Application modules (surface prep, drain, tank/vessel, etc. — provider catalogues vary)  
- Hands-on under approved instructors  

### 3. Competence carding
- Safety Awareness + practical module(s) + workplace evidence pathways described publicly  
- Digital / photo card systems used by association and providers  

### Refresher
- Safety Awareness often **retaken every ~3 years** for continued registration (provider public notes; confirm with WJA for current rule)
