## PPE checklist — extract

Minimum PPE: hard hat, safety glasses, face shield, hearing protection, slicker suit (FRC), task-appropriate gloves (PVC), metatarsal boots.  
**Double hearing protection if working within 35 feet of the pump.**  
Additional: pressure-rated garment when required.

**MCR link:** Hearing / double-hearing within ~35 ft of pump (WJTA) — align with OPS-P-019 and Claude gap G2.

## Manual shotgun checklist — operational sequence (summary)

**Hand signals:** Turn on water · Pressure up / OK to blast · Cut pressure  

**Pressure up:** Demonstrate hand signals → lock down gun barrel until working pressure → maintain trigger until pump set → test dump valve.  

**Safe operation:** Approved shotgun length + hose shroud; stance; torso control; whip hose; dump not leaking; standoff; clean ~12–18" squares.  

**Pressure down:** Release trigger → signal pump shut down with LOS → do not leave gun until pump fully down.
