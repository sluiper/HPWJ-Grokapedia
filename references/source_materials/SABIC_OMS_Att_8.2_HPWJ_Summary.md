## Method selection (hierarchy — critical)

1. Prefer **safe alternate methods** instead of HPWJ where reasonably practicable (process + asset engineering SME).  
2. Prefer **designated safe area** over in-situ jetting where practicable.  
3. Method risk matrix ranks fully automated (controls outside) as lowest risk; flexible lance without hose catcher as **Major**.

| Method class | Risk posture (HP jet injury) |
|--------------|------------------------------|
| Fully automated, controls **outside** jetting area | Insignificant |
| Mechanized, controls outside (>6 m or physical shielding) | Insignificant |
| Mechanized, controls **inside** jetting area | Minor |
| Manual with strain relief (no reaction force) inside area | Minor–Significant |
| Manual without strain relief (exposed to reaction force) | Significant |
| Flexible lance **with** hose catcher | Significant |
| Flexible lance **without** hose catcher / open-ended hose | **Major** |

**Encyclopedia use:** Aligns with OPS-P-019 preference for auto/semi-auto and MCR manual-gate rules.
