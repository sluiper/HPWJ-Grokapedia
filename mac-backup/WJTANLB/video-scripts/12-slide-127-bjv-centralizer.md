## Instructor notes from the slide

Start the **anti-reversal device (ARD)** discussion.

Overall rigid length here is **well beyond compliance** (in a good way). The centralizer’s job is to **center** the tooling. Do **not** assume that using a centralizer means you have the right overall rigid length — though there is a good chance you got there because centralizers are long.

Centralizers will **not** like bends or 90° elbows. That is covered later. No other critical points on this slide.
