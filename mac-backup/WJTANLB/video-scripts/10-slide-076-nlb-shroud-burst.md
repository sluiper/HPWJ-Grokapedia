## What this video is

Shop-floor burst test of an **orange NLB-style lance/hose shroud** on concrete. Hose inside the orange sleeve fails; water is contained and dumps out the ends instead of cutting the operator.

## Visual script

1. Orange shroud on the floor, slightly wrinkled, couplings at both ends, metal lance/hose to the right.
2. Pressure: shroud balloons / twists.
3. Water dumps from the left end — burst contained inside the sleeve.

## Instructor notes / slide text

Shroud length should be a **minimum of 6 feet**, stainless-steel braid or ballistic-grade fabric, resistant to oil, chemicals and abrasion.

**Critical:** the shroud must cover any connections or hose in close proximity to the operator and be fitted with a method to keep it in place while in use.

Must be manufacturer-tested and verified. Any shroud that has contained a high-pressure hose burst must be **removed from service and tagged** until a competent person inspects it. Most that have contained a burst are **not repairable** and should be destroyed.
