## What this video is

TST pressure-rated garment **tap / swipe test** (CE verification, 2022). Lab tech lays a red fabric sample, jet passes, sample is pulled to show whether it cut through. Ends on TST branding.

## On-screen script (the actual words in the video)

## Instructor notes from the slide

This video **is** impactful, but the instructor **must** say:

- Any cut-resistant garment is rated **very particularly**.
- Communicate the exact **pressure, flow, orifice(s), standoff, and traverse/swipe speed** used in the tap test.
- Unless the accident happens in **this exact fashion**, you **cannot** rely on the garment to protect you, your life, or your safety.

## Review points

- On-screen numbers are the rating envelope, not a field guarantee.
- End-card marketing (“you’re safe with us”) conflicts with WJTA teaching — call that out.
