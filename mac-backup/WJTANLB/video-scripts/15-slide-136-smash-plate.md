## What this video is

Field demo of a **smash plate** (round plate covering the pipe mouth) with the lance hose passing through a central guide. Operator in yellow slicker. Effluent dumps out a lower window. Shape name: `trashcan awd fixed`.

## Visual script

1. Close-up: circular steel plate on the pipe end, two rectangular windows, hose through the centre collet, water pouring from the bottom window.
2. Operator in yellow, gloved, holding the hose; second hose across the plate.
3. Plate covers the full bore so a reversing nozzle cannot exit and hit the crew, but **does not** blank the pipe — effluent has a path out.

## Slide text / notes

The smash plate provides additional protection to prevent the nozzle from exiting the pipe in the event of a reversal. Design covers the entire cross-section **yet provides a pathway for material to exit**.

Instructor: you don’t want to cover the entire end of the pipe — leave a space for effluent. **9.10.7**

## Review points

- Smash plate ≠ complete blank. Leave effluent path.
- Complements ARD / stinger (videos 13–14), does not replace them.
