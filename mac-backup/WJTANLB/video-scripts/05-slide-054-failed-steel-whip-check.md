## What this video is

Lab test in a red safety cage. Blue 20 mm hose across the box. Thin **steel** whip-check / lanyard. Coupling fails; the hose end **is not restrained** and flies.

## Visual script

1. Setup: blue hose through the cage, small steel cable/whip from left fitting, slack hanging.
2. Burst / disconnect event: mist fills the box.
3. Failed fitting / hose end whips freely toward the right wall — the steel check did not catch it.
