## What this video is

Low-res field clip. Two workers on a wet grated deck / air-preheater or similar bundle. Title in the deck: **WHAT’S WRONG HERE?** Shape name: `air_preheater_manual.5hose_noAWD`.

Play it and **let the class call the faults**. Do not read the list first.

## Visual script

- Operator in foreground: light-coloured coveralls, feeding a hose from a floor device; **no anti-withdrawal device** on the lance.
- Second operator: white/Tyvek-style chem suit, green hood, **face shield up**, working the sheet.
- Stinger-looking rigid section is present but **not** a proper AWD.
- Wet grating, dark, no foot guard between operator and nozzle.
- Multiple hoses, informal setup.

## Instructor notes from the slide

Things someone should identify:

- PPE: Tyvek / chem suits that soak through to skin — not water-jet PPE.
- **No anti-withdrawal device** (most obvious). Stinger is not sufficient.
- No guard between the person’s foot and the nozzle assembly.
- Face shield up.
- “The list goes on.” There is no anti-withdrawal at all.

## Review points

- Pedagogue: **solicit**, don’t prescribe.
- Keep this as a case study, not as a “how we do it” clip.
