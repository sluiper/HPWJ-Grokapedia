## What this video is

WJTA “what right looks like / what wrong looks like” montage. Red warning panels vs green check panels, over a music bed. Used at the **start of the deck** so the class sees real jobs before any theory.

## On-screen script (titles / labels)

1. **LINE MOLEING** — red panel + hazard triangle. Operator in yellow rain gear at a flanged line; steam/spray leaking at the flange; overlay `#ECO`.
2. **SURFACE PREPARATION** — red panel + hazard triangle. Shotgun lance on a rusty vessel/flange; lots of mist; a helmet sitting on the equipment instead of on a head.
3. **TUBE CLEANING** — red panel + hazard triangle. Phone/TikTok clip (`@deerhunting_01`): worker on a tube-sheet with yellow Tyvek, green hose, torch-like light on the sheet. Manual, informal, not a controlled HPWJ setup.
4. **TUBE CLEANING** — green panel + check mark. Automated / fixture-mounted lances on a bundle, water contained, operators off the line of fire.
5. End card (asset-owner training):

> **WHAT RIGHT LOOKS LIKE!**  
> Safety is everyone’s responsibility.  
> Operators, Permit Issuers/Writers, Permit Auditors, and HSE Professionals  
> [need] the knowledge to identify current best practices and compliance.

## Instructor notes from the slide

Start at the top: various organizations exist worldwide. Other people have this same problem — water jetting is not as standardized as welding. Frank’s line: *you see a welding rig driving down the road, it’s a welding rig. I’ve had several times where folks drive up next to me at a light with a pump: “What’s that????”*

Global Industrial Cleaning Coalition — adopt 7 basic principles. Associations: WJTA, EWJI, SIR (Benelux), S3C (France), WJA (UK), DIRV (Germany), ICAC (China), KWJA (Korea), AUSJET (Australia), WJTSJ (Japan). Reason: crews work in multiple countries; other standards exist; for this course we follow WJTA.
