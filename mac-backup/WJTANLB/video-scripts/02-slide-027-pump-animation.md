## What this video is

3D cutaway of a **positive-displacement reciprocating plunger pump** (Uraca-style triplex): crankshaft, connecting rods, three plungers, fluid-end valves, water flow shown as blue streamlines.

## Visual script (shot order)

1. Exterior of the power end / crankcase.
2. Cutaway: three vertical plunger assemblies over the crank.
3. Fluid-end section: plunger stroking, **suction ball valve** opening, water filling the chamber.
4. Discharge stroke: **discharge ball valve** lifting, water leaving toward the discharge manifold.
5. Repeat so the class can see “this pump displaces water.”

## Instructor notes from the slide

Shows function of the pump so the VT can say what is happening all the time, and how the crew is manipulating / controlling / forcing the water to create different “on or off” effects.

Every instructor should at least be able to say, during this short clip: **this pump is reciprocating and displacing water, and what we do with it is on the other end.**

- MF: keep pump details to a minimum — limited for the purpose of the course.
- JH: focus is nozzle-operator safety and operations, but keep the “know where the water is gonna go” theory in mind.
