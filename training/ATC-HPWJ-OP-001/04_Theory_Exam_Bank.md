## Section A — Multiple choice (2 marks each unless noted)

**A1.** Competence for Anabeeb HPWJ is best described as:  
a) Holding any certificate from any country  
b) Ability to prevent injection injuries and apply MCR controls under real conditions  
c) Years of service alone  
d) Client verbal approval only  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-030 / Ch13  

**A2.** Pre-use inspection (MCR-023) is:  
a) Optional if the last shift already checked  
b) Mandatory documented inspection before use and each shift; permit prerequisite  
c) Required only for UHP  
d) Replaced by a verbal “looks good”  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-023 (RPN priority)  

**A3.** Flex lance without correct AWD / catcher is:  
a) Allowed for short jobs  
b) Allowed if supervisor watches  
c) Not permitted  
d) Allowed below a pressure the operator chooses  
**Answer:** c **Map:** MCR-019/020  

**A4.** When line of sight between lance and pump is lost, Anabeeb requires (MCR-047):  
a) One person total is enough  
b) Two people minimum always, no extra role  
c) Minimum three competent persons with one dedicated to emergency stop/dump  
d) Radio only, no dedicated person  
**Answer:** c **Map:** MCR-047  

**A5.** Rupture disc rating for Anabeeb systems (MCR-048):  
a) Any disc that fits  
b) Rated no greater than 1.2× MAWP of the lowest-rated component  
c) Always equal to pump nameplate only  
d) Optional if dump valve exists  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-048  

**A6.** Unauthorised persons near Anabeeb operating equipment (MCR-051):  
a) Allowed inside 5 m if wearing PPE  
b) Must stay outside the distance stated in MCR-051; system depressurised on breach  
c) Allowed if they are managers  
d) Only a problem offshore  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-051  

**A7.** Hand-held shotgunning limits for Anabeeb are defined in:  
a) Operator preference  
b) MCR-050 (pressure, orifice, and reaction-force absolute)  
c) Manufacturer sticker only  
d) Client toolbox talk only  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-050  

**A8.** Lance/hose tip marking requirement is stated in:  
a) MCR-052  
b) MCR-001 only  
c) MCR-033 only  
d) There is no tip mark rule  
**Answer:** a **Map:** MCR-052  

**A9.** Maximum Anabeeb HPWJ system pressure ceiling is stated in:  
a) MCR-046  
b) MCR-012  
c) MCR-061  
d) MCR-029  
**Answer:** a **Map:** MCR-046  

**A10.** Whip checks are required:  
a) On one side of the connection only  
b) Both sides of every HP connection, correct WLL, inspected every use  
c) Only on flexible lances  
d) Only above 20k psi  
**Answer:** b **Map:** MCR-006  

**A11.** The preferred hierarchy of methods when reasonably practicable is:  
a) Manual first  
b) Automated/mechanised methods with operators outside the jetting area

**A12.** Reaction force formulas (imperial and metric) for Anabeeb encyclopedia use are controlled by:

**A13.** The three additive reaction-force controls are defined in:

**Answer:** a **Map:** MCR-016  

**A14.** Dump / dead-man system (MCR-010):
