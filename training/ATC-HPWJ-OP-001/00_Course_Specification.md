## 2. Entry gates

Learners must meet **before** unsupervised field operation under this certificate (assessment may run in parallel with employment onboarding):

- Fit for HPWJ work per company medical fitness practice (Ch13 §13.8 / site HR).  
- Literate in course language of delivery (or approved translator arrangement).  
- Site induction / PTW awareness as required by host site.  
- Willingness to exercise stop-work authority (MCR-038).

**Not an entry gate for the classroom:** prior unsupervised HPWJ operation (course is the path to Level 2). Manual high-risk work still requires MCR-049 experience + management approval before field manual lancing/gun work.

## 6. Assessment design rules

- Minimum **30%** of theory marks on high-priority MCR (including Anabeeb 047–052).  
- Minimum **20%** of theory marks on injection injury recognition & response (Ch10 / Ch24).  
- Reaction-force exam items must require the learner to **look up MCR-017** (or provided MCR extract card) — trainers must not print a free-floating constant.  
- Practical pass/fail only via controlled checklist.
