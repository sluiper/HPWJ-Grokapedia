# ATC-HPWJ-SUP — Trainer Guide  
## دليل المدرب — المشرف

**MCR Controls Referenced:** MCR-023, 024, 025, 028, 030, 036, 038, 039, 041, 042, 043, 047–052, 049  

**Class size:** Max 6–8 (more scenario time)  
**Prerequisite check:** Verify OP-001 (or equivalent) before Day 1 practicals.

---

## Absolute trainer rules
1. RF: formulas only from **MCR-017** — supervisors must model lookup discipline for crews.  
2. Four Pre-Job Gates are non-negotiable — no “soft start” scenarios that skip gates.  
3. Project Manager non-delegable duty (OPS-P-019 / Ch12.1) must be taught accurately.  
4. Cite MCR-IDs when stating Anabeeb absolutes.

---

## Day 1 — Accountability & Pre-Job Gates (~8 h)

| Block | Min | Content |
|-------|-----|---------|
| 0 | 20 | Safety; role of Level 4 vs Level 2 |
| 1 | 50 | Role hierarchy table (Ch12.1): PM, Site Supervisor, HSE, Authorising Person, Dump operator |
| 2 | 40 | MCR-036 accountability; MCR-041 client Authorising Person (SABIC context) |
| 3 | 60 | **Gate 1 — Competency** (MCR-030/042/043/047/049) workshop |
| 4 | 50 | **Gate 2 — Documentation** (TS, JSA, hierarchy MCR-039, permit path) |
| 5 | 70 | **Gate 3 — Equipment & site** deep pre-use leadership (MCR-023/024 + 046–052) |
| 6 | 50 | **Gate 4 — Briefing** (MCR-025): toolbox structure; RF via MCR-017; heat MCR-033 |
| 7 | 40 | Tabletop: refuse permit until all gates closed |
| 8 | 30 | Day 1 formative quiz |

### Gate 1 coaching points
- Automatic qualification ≠ manual (MCR-049 / Ch12).  
- Team of three + dedicated dump when LOS lost (MCR-047).  
- Medical Alert on every crew member (MCR-043).

### Gate 3 coaching points
- Supervisor **spot-checks** (MCR-024) — not just paperwork.  
- Disc ≤1.2× lowest MAWP (MCR-048); tip mark (MCR-052); exclusion (MCR-051).  
- Flex without AWD = Not Permitted (MCR-020).

### Scenario bank (use 3+)
1. Client pressure to start with incomplete pre-use.  
2. Manual lancing requested without MCR-049 evidence.  
3. Unauthorised person inside exclusion (MCR-051).  
4. SIMOPS hot work adjacent to jetting.  
5. Supervisor tempted to enter line of fire to “help.”
