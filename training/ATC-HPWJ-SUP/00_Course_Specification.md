### Learning outcomes / مخرجات التعلم
1. Execute the **four Pre-Job Gates** (competency, documentation, equipment/site, briefing) before any pressurisation (Ch12.2).  
2. Verify competency, Medical Alert, team of three (MCR-030/042/043/047); manual experience gate (MCR-049).  
3. Lead pre-use quality and supervisor spot-checks (MCR-023/024).  
4. Control SIMOPS interfaces (MCR-028) and stop-work culture (MCR-038).  
5. Enforce hierarchy of methods (MCR-039) and Anabeeb absolutes (046–052).  
6. Coordinate emergency response leadership (Ch10).
