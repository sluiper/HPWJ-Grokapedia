### Trainer rules
- Do **not** teach RF formula constants for calculation competence — awareness of “reaction force can throw the operator” is enough; operators use MCR-017.  
- Do **not** issue Operator certificates.  
- Show App G and App D as display copies.  
- Model: unauthorised persons stay outside the line of fire (SF 18/20 lesson / MCR-057 principle for supervisors).  

### Demo props
Barricade tape, measuring tape for MCR-051 line, sample Medical Alert card, Never Rules one-pager.
