### Outcomes
1. Apply **every** MCR-034 control before pressure.  
2. Install/verify AWD (MCR-019/020); tip mark (MCR-052); vacuum at outlet.  
3. Size exclusion for UHP band (MCR-031); unauthorised rule (MCR-051).  
4. Calculate RF using **MCR-017**; enforce MCR-016.  
5. Prefer automated/mechanised methods where reasonably practicable (MCR-039).  
6. Lead emergency dump and injection response under UHP conditions.
